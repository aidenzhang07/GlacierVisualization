Platform features

Built from the science up.

🗺

Interactive world map

215,000 glacier outlines rendered on a full-screen Leaflet map with satellite, OSM, and topo base layers. Click any polygon for its full record.

📅

2D time-slider

Step through a glacier's GLIMS polygon observations year by year. Watch the outline contract over decades on a live satellite base map.

🏔

3D terrain viewer

Real Landsat and Sentinel-2 imagery draped over elevation-displaced terrain. Orbit, zoom, and play through 40 years of annual composites.

📊

Mass balance charts

Annual accumulation vs ablation bars, cumulative loss lines, decadal averages, and acceleration curves for all 62 WGMS reference glaciers.

🔬

Unified data model

GLIMS, WGMS, GlaThiDa, and ITS_LIVE linked by glacier ID. Cross-database identifiers surfaced on every detail page.

📡

Live satellite fetch

Google Earth Engine integration generates cloud-free annual composites on demand for any glacier coordinate worldwide.
